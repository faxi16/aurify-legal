Aurify - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 9, 2026

Welcome to Aurify ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us when using our Augmented Reality (AR) memory placement and spatial audio platform.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with our AR features, we request access to certain device capabilities and collect minimal required information:

  • Camera Access: Used exclusively to display Augmented Reality (AR) experiences, calculate surface planes, and allow you to capture photos/reference images. Video frames are processed locally on your device for AR tracking.
  • Location Data (GPS): Used to anchor AR memories to exact physical coordinates and display nearby digital memories on the map. Fine and coarse location data are collected only while using the application.
  • Microphone Access: Used only when you choose to record spatial audio notes or voice memories.
  • Account Data: When you register, we collect your email address, chosen username, and optional profile details via our authentication provider (Supabase / Firebase).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To render AR content in your physical environment.
  • To display nearby memories on the interactive map view.
  • To manage your user account, authentication, and social features (following, comments, likes).
  • To send important push notifications regarding account activity or social interactions (via Firebase Cloud Messaging).

3. Third-Party Services

Aurify integrates with trusted third-party service providers for infrastructure and features:

  • Google Maps & ARCore: For mapping and AR surface tracking.
  • Firebase: For push notification delivery.
  • Supabase: For cloud database storage, media storage, and user authentication.

4. Data Retention and Security

All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS protocols. Your uploaded memories and profile data are stored securely. You can delete your memories or request account deletion at any time within the app or by contacting us.

5. Children's Privacy

Aurify is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
