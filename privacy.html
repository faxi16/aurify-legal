Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 23, 2026

Aurify ("We", "Our") is dedicated to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy in compliance with applicable data protection regulations including the GDPR and global standards.

1. Information We Collect and Purposes

Data Category Purpose
Precise Location (GPS) Anchoring digital memories at real-world coordinates and showing nearby memories on map/AR view.
Camera & Visuals AR scene understanding, plane detection, tracking reference images, and uploading media.
Microphone Recording audio when creating voice-recorded memories.
Account Info Username, email, profile details, and encrypted authentication tokens.
Device & Diagnostics Device model, OS version, crash logs, and security telemetry.

2. Camera and AR Processing Privacy

Our AR features (powered by ARCore) process real-time camera frames locally on your device to detect surfaces and track anchor points. Raw video streams are never transmitted or stored on our servers unless you explicitly capture and publish a photo or video.

3. Location Data Usage

Location data is accessed only when using location-specific features. We do not sell or share your precise location data with third-party advertisers.

4. Data Storage & Account Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

You have full control over your personal data. You can delete your account and all associated memories permanently at any time via Profile → Edit Profile → Delete Account or by contacting support.

5. Contact Us

For any privacy inquiries or data requests, please use the in-app Feedback & Support feature.
