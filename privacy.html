Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Aurify values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Location Data (GPS): Used to anchor memories in the real world and discover nearby content.
  • Camera & Visuals: Used for AR rendering, surface tracking, and uploading photos.
  • Microphone: Used when creating voice-recorded memories.
  • Account Information: Username, email, and credentials for account authentication.

2. Data Security & Deletion

Your data is encrypted and securely stored. We never sell your personal information. You can request complete deletion of your account and associated memories at any time via the app settings.

3. Contact

If you have any questions, please reach out through the in-app support center.
