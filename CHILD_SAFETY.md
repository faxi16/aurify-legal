## 1. Zero Tolerance Commitment
Aurify maintains an absolute zero-tolerance policy regarding **Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)** and **Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE)**. Any depiction, promotion, facilitation, or sharing of explicit or harmful material involving minors is strictly forbidden across all app services, including Augmented Reality (AR) spatial memories, AI-generated 3D models, photos, voice notes, and user profiles.

## 2. Automated Moderation & Pre-Upload Screening
- **Skin & Nudity Detection:** All user-uploaded media files are screened by automated moderation filters prior to publication or 3D reconstruction. Media exhibiting explicit content or potential nudity is rejected and blocked immediately.
- **Generative AI Guardrails:** Generative text-to-3D and photo-to-3D features enforce strict ethical safety boundaries that reject sensitive or inappropriate prompts and source images.

## 3. In-App Reporting Tools
Every post, spatial memory, and user profile contains an accessible **Report (Bildir)** mechanism. Users can report content under categories such as *Inappropriate Content*, *Harassment*, or *Harmful Behavior*. Reported items are immediately queued for review and hidden pending verification.

## 4. Enforcement & Reporting to Authorities
When any confirmed instance of child exploitation or abusive material is identified:
- The offensive content is permanently and immediately deleted from all servers and caches.
- The offending account is permanently terminated and banned from future access.
- Confirmed violations are reported to law enforcement agencies and relevant international organizations, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).
