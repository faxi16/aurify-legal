Safety Policy

Child Safety Standards & CSAE Prevention Policy

Application: Aurify (com.maherlabbad.aura) • Effective Date: October 2026
Zero-Tolerance Commitment: Aurify strictly prohibits any form of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE). We enforce an absolute zero-tolerance standard across all app features, including Augmented Reality (AR) spatial memories, AI-generated 3D models, photos, voice notes, and user profiles.

1. Policy Overview and Scope

Aurify is committed to providing a safe, creative, and respectful environment. Any content that depicts, encourages, promotes, or facilitates child sexual abuse, exploitation, grooming, or harm is strictly banned and will be immediately purged.

2. Automated Moderation & Screening

We deploy proactive technical measures to prevent harmful material from reaching our platform:

  • Automated Nudity & NSFW Filtering: User-submitted photos and textures undergo automated content moderation filters prior to processing and publication. Media containing explicit nudity or sensitive content is blocked immediately.
  • AI Generation Guardrails: The generative AI 3D engine strictly forbids prompts and source imagery related to minors, nudity, or inappropriate depictions.

3. In-App Reporting Tools

Aurify provides direct in-app reporting tools on every post, spatial memory, and user profile:

  • Users can instantly report any post or profile under categories such as Inappropriate Content, Harassment, or Harmful Behavior.
  • Reported items are automatically prioritized for review and provisionally hidden pending verification.

4. Enforcement and Law Enforcement Cooperation

Upon detection of any violation regarding child exploitation:

  • Immediate Removal: The offensive material is permanently erased from all production databases and storage caches.
  • Permanent Account Termination: The offending account is permanently banned, with device/IP-level blacklisting applied.
  • Legal Reporting: In compliance with international standards, instances of CSAM/CSAE are reported to law enforcement agencies and organizations such as the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).
